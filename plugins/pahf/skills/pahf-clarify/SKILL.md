# PAHF Pre-Action Clarification

Before acting, explicitly state your understanding of the situation and check for hidden assumptions.

## Steps

1. **Restate the request** in your own words — what is actually being asked?
2. **State your intended approach** — what will you do and why?
3. **Surface assumptions** — what are you assuming that you haven't confirmed?
4. **Check for conflicts** — does this request touch any known principles from memory?
5. **Confirm or ask** — if any assumption is load-bearing, surface it before proceeding.

## Format

Keep it tight. One or two sentences per step. This is not a long monologue — it is a focused checkpoint.

```
Understanding: [what you think is being asked]
Approach: [what you plan to do]
Assumptions: [what you're assuming]
Conflicts: [any principle conflicts from memory, or "none"]
```

If there are no load-bearing assumptions and no conflicts, proceed immediately after stating the above.
If there is a conflict or ambiguous assumption, stop and ask.

## When to Use

- Before writing significant code
- Before making a design decision
- Before taking an action with side effects (push, deploy, delete)
- When the request is underspecified
